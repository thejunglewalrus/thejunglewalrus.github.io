Multi-crit, support QOL, and a small balance patch

  • Crit overcap becomes multi-crit: at 120% crit rate, a multi-crit procs 20% of the time (shown in red, 100 → 200 → 300).Lee1:15:57
  • A balance patch goes with it, because multi-crit alone gives some classes about 10% more damage.Lee1:22:28
  • Bard Sonic Vibration and Artist Sunwell now last for the full duration after touching them once.Lee1:20:04
  • Paladin and Valkyrie AP buff cooldowns are equaled, and Brand lasts about 50% longer.Lee1:20:04
  • These are targeted fixes. The larger all-class balance patch is saved for the winter update.Lee1:24:07

The first real test of the new director. "About 10%" was said out loud, not shown on a slide, so check the numbers on patch day.
