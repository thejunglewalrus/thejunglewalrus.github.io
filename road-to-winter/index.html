The road to winter

Every promise from Smilegate's Oct 29 broadcast, in the order it comes due. Outgoing director Jeon Jae-hak called himself "a guilty man". This is what Lee Byung-tak now has to deliver.

Multi-crit, support convenience and a balance pass

  • Crit overcap becomes multi-crit: at 120% crit rate, a multi-crit procs 20% of the time (shown in red, 100 → 200 → 300).Lee1:15:57
  • A balance pass goes with it, because multi-crit alone gives some classes about 10% more damage.Lee1:22:28
  • Bard and Artist field attack buffs stay on after one entry.Lee1:20:04
  • Paladin and Valkyrie attack-buff cooldowns are equalised, and Brand lasts about 50% longer.Lee1:20:04
  • These are local fixes. The biggest all-class balance improvement is saved for the winter update.Lee1:24:07

The first real test of the new director. "About 10%" was said out loud, not shown on a slide, so check the numbers on patch day.
